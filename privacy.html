Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

AYRA is a free, on-device voice assistant for Android and Windows. This policy explains, in plain words, what AYRA does with your information: nothing leaves your device.

1. No data collection

AYRA collects, stores, uploads, or shares no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app makes no network requests of its own.

2. On-device processing

Voice recognition and text-to-speech use your device’s built-in, on-device services. Your voice commands are processed on your phone or PC to carry out the command you gave (for example, opening an app or setting a timer). Voice audio is not sent to AYRA’s developer or to any server by AYRA itself.

3. What stays on your device

  • Notes and reminders you create — stored only on your device, and deleted when you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Things you ask AYRA to remember — stored only on your device.
  • Your ON/OFF choice for the background assistant — stored only on your device.

4. Permissions AYRA uses — and why

  • Microphone — to hear your voice commands, including “Hey AYRA” while the background assistant is ON.
  • Notifications — to show the persistent “AYRA is running” notification while the background assistant is ON (Android requires this).
  • Draw over other apps — to show the floating mic button above other apps while the background assistant is ON.
  • Ignore battery optimisation — so Android does not stop the background assistant while it is ON. You can turn this off any time in your phone’s settings.
  • Run at startup — to restart the background assistant after a phone restart, only if it was ON when the phone shut down.

Turning the assistant OFF stops all background activity. Uninstalling the app removes everything AYRA stored.

5. Third-party services you ask AYRA to open

When you ask AYRA to open a website or app (for example YouTube, Google, or WhatsApp), your device connects directly to that service. Those services have their own privacy policies, which AYRA does not control.

6. Children

AYRA collects no data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or about AYRA can be raised on the project page: github.com/Muzammilarain44/Ayra.
